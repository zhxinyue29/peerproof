## ⚠️ 动手前必须解决的一件事:剧本的经济模型和产品不一样

剧本写的是:

> 主办方出 **2,000 MON 奖励池**,参与者每次验证到场 **赚 10 MON**。

**产品不是这样的,而且这是您自己定过的边界。**

PeerProof 的钱是这样走的:

| | 剧本 | 产品真实逻辑 |
|---|---|---|
| 钱从哪来 | 主办方掏奖励池 | **参与者自己交押金** |
| 谁拿到钱 | 到场的人拿主办方的钱 | 到场的人**拿回自己的押金**,再**瓜分没来的人被没收的押金** |
| 主办方 | 出钱、管钱 | **一分钱都碰不到**(合约里根本没有让他收钱或放钱的函数) |

「主办方奖金池」在 `docs/设计稿/README.md` 里被明确列为**不做**,理由写的是
"和「主办方从头到尾碰不到一分钱」冲突"。而"主办方碰不到钱"正是这个项目在黑客松上
唯一的立论。

**剧本自己也写了**:"Do not pretend an unsupported feature exists."
按剧本拍,整支片子的经济故事是假的,而且假在最核心的地方 —— 评委一点合约就穿帮。

**建议的改法**(只改说法,不改镜头、不改时长):

| 原台词/字幕 | 改成 |
|---|---|
| "…and fund rewards for people who really participate." | "…and set the stake that makes showing up mean something." |
| 通知卡 `10 MON Reward` | `0.5 MON stake · no-shows pay the room` |
| "And earn." | "And get paid by the people who didn't come." |
| 结尾链路 `Organizer → Matching → Real Participation → Monad Reward` | `Stake → Show up → The room vouches → The contract pays` |
| Dashboard `Rewards distributed 1,280/2,000 MON` | `Deposits held 1,420 MON · forfeited by no-shows 140 MON` |

另外两个剧本里有、产品里没有的:

- **`92% Match` / "Matching"** —— 没有匹配引擎。剧本自己标了"如果产品没有匹配分数,剪掉"
- **`Interested 236`** —— 没有"感兴趣"这个指标。漏斗只有 报名 → 已验证

**这一条没定之前,A 组里唯一受影响的是第 7 镜的通知卡文字**(那是后期字幕,不是 H3 画的),
所以 **A 组可以照常开工**,不必等。

### A9 和片头 Logo、片尾字卡:不要交给 H3

**H3 写不出字。** 画面里但凡出现字母就是乱码 —— 这台机器上反复验证过。
凡是有可读文字的镜头(片头 Logo 三行字、A9 的四段链路、片尾 slogan)一律
**SVG + CSS 排时序**,不走视频模型。

这条不只是为了字:上一次想让视频模型排"有先后顺序"的动作,实测帧差 0.09%,
它只会慢慢推镜,给不了"先 A 后 B 再 C"。四段链路正是这种需求。

## H3 的四条硬约束(本机踩出来的,别再试)

### 1. 画面里一个字都不许有

每条提示词都要带 `NO_TEXT` 那一段。老板电脑屏、手机屏、现场签到牌 ——
**全部只画抽象光形和渐变**,真实 UI 后期合成上去。
剧本里写的"屏幕角落可见 `Monad Community Night / Registrations 24`"不能让 H3 画。

### 2. 跨场景的脸必漂 —— 但用户 2026-09-20 定了:**不必是同一个人,画风一致就行**

这条一松,整批的拍法跟着变:**脸可以给了**。

原来 a2 写成"三分之三背侧只露半张脸",是为了躲脸的漂移。既然不要求同一个人,
那一层就没必要了 —— 而且它伤故事:a2 的戏眼就是那一声叹气,a5 的戏眼就是脸
一下子亮起来,都得看见脸。**a2 第二批重拍成正面。**

留着不改的是 a7a/a8 的背影 —— 那个不是为了躲脸,是"走进会场"本来就该从背后拍。

**画风一致靠什么守住**(这才是现在要交付的):

1. **STYLE 前缀** —— 每条提示词都带同一段:深蓝靛底、紫与品红主光、薄荷绿点缀、
   Pixar 风圆润造型、体积雾、浅景深。这一段是稳的
2. **网页的核心画面本来就是这条 H3 管线出的** —— 首页 hero 视频、cover-1 和
   `peerproof_scene2/e` 是同一个场景同一块大屏。会场镜头天然接得上
3. **同一序列内部用尾帧接首帧**,人物必然一致

**做不到、也不用做的**:复刻 `peers-hero` / `joined-banner` 上那个戴鸭舌帽的男孩。
那几张不是 H3 出的(身上烤着可读英文,H3 写不出字),仓库里也没有生成脚本。

### 2b. 老的规避手法(留档)

同一个参与者要出现在 A2 / A5 / A7 / A8 四个镜头里。H3 跨场景保不住脸。

三个已验证的解法,优先级从高到低:

1. **背对镜头 / 越肩 / 只拍手和手机** —— 不给正脸就没有漂移
   (A7、A8 直接这么拍;A2 可以拍侧脸+手机)
2. **同一连续镜头切两段**时,把前一段的**尾帧**喂给 `first_frame`,接得住
   (A5 接 A2、A6 接 A5、A8 接 A7 都用这个)
3. **跨场景硬接尾帧会在前 1–2 秒 morph 成照片重影** —— 所以 A2→A5 之间要
   确认是"同一个人同一个沙发同一时刻",不是换场

### 3. 参考图 ≠ 目标画风

别把 PeerProof 首页那张图当画风参考喂进去 —— 参考图会变成**开场画风**,
前 5 秒必糊成照片重影。要锚画风,用 **H3 自己已经生成好的成品帧**。
现有可用成品在 `~/ComfyUI-H3/output/peerproof_scene2/e_00001_.mp4`(用户选定过的那一镜)。

### 4. 只锁机位,不要锁人

"不许动"写宽了会把人一起冻住 —— 上一轮实测相邻帧差 0.236,像幻灯片。
要写成:**机位锁死,但灯在扫、雾在飘、屏在呼吸、人在动**。

## 跑作业的方式(不能直接跑)

H3 装权重吃 **36GB 内存 + 16GB 显存**,这台机器 62GB/24GB,
两个大作业同时活着会换页,实测同一镜头 759s → 3075s 最后卡死。

```bash
NEED_GB=40 NEED_VRAM_GB=18 RELEASE=comfyui-h3.service \
  ~/bin/大活排队.sh <标签> <脚本>
```

长作业必须 systemd 托管,`nohup`/`setsid` 都挡不住会话回收:

```bash
systemd-run --user --unit=peerproof-a1 --collect \
  ~/bin/大活排队.sh a1 ~/文档/Monad_Circle/scripts/demo片/run_a1.sh
```

**判完成看产出文件,不看 `systemctl is-active`。** 本机被"服务 active 但端口返 000"
骗过四次。服务起来要 75 秒以上,权重装满要 11 分钟 —— 已经活着就复用,别重启。

数帧用 `/home/liyakun/bin/ffmpeg`,**不要用 `ffprobe`**(PATH 上那个是包装脚本,
会把好文件判成坏的)。

## A 组提示词(英文,直接进脚本)

公共前缀,每条都带:

```
STYLE  = cinematic 3D animated film render, Pixar-style stylised characters with soft rounded
         shapes, warm skin tones. Deep navy and indigo environment, strong violet and magenta
         light, small glints of mint green. Volumetric haze, shallow depth of field.

NO_TEXT= There is absolutely no text anywhere in the frame: no letters, no words, no numbers,
         no logos, no signs, no banners, no captions. Every screen shows only an abstract
         glowing gradient and soft geometric shapes.

ALIVE  = The camera move is the only camera move; everything else in the room is alive —
         light shifts, haze drifts, screens pulse, the character breathes and shifts weight.
```

### A1 · 办公室(5s)

```
A modern startup office at night, deep navy walls, one large monitor as the main light source
washing violet across the desk and the man's face. A stylised young man in a dark shirt sits at
the desk, leaning forward on one elbow, looking at the monitor with a tired, worried expression
— not angry, just deflated. He exhales, rubs the back of his neck, and turns his head slightly
to look off-camera as if asking someone a question. Slow gentle dolly-in on his face.
```
锚点:紫色显示器主光 + 疲惫不是暴怒。座右边留空,后期可以贴真实 UI。

### A2 · 沙发(5s)

```
A dim living room at night, deep navy and indigo, a single warm lamp behind the sofa. A stylised
young person lies sprawled sideways across a low sofa, head on a cushion, holding a phone above
their face with one hand. The phone's glow is the key light on their cheek. They flick the screen
twice with a thumb, let the hand drop onto their chest, and sigh with their eyes closed. Camera
holds in a slow static medium shot, three-quarters from behind the sofa back.
```
锚点:**三分之三背侧角度**,脸只露一小半 —— 为后面三个镜头省掉脸的一致性。

### A4 · 光线穿城(5s)

```
A single bright violet ribbon of light bursts out of a glowing monitor and flies out through a
window into a stylised night city of deep navy towers, threading between the buildings, leaving a
soft magenta trail. It curves down towards a phone lying on a sofa in a warm-lit window. Fast
flying camera following the ribbon from behind, motion blur, volumetric haze.
```
锚点:**一条光,不是一群粒子**。H3 对"一个主体做一件事"最听话。

### A5 · 坐起来(6s)

首帧 = A2 尾帧。

```
Unbroken continuation of the same shot. The same person on the same sofa: their phone suddenly
brightens, throwing violet light across their face. Their eyes snap open, they push up onto one
elbow and then sit bolt upright, both hands now on the phone, leaning in towards it with sudden
interest. The camera pushes in gently as they rise.
```

### A6 · 推进手机(2s)

首帧 = A5 尾帧。

```
Unbroken continuation. The camera pushes straight in towards the phone held in both hands until
the glowing screen fills the entire frame and everything else falls out of focus. The screen is a
pure abstract violet gradient with soft geometric shapes and no text.
```
最后满屏那一帧要**干净的紫色渐变** —— 后期在这一帧上无缝切真实录屏。

### A7a · 走进会场(5s)

```
Inside a stylised event venue at night. A huge stage screen at the back is by far the brightest
thing in frame and lights the whole room: violet and magenta wash over the floor, thick haze and
a sparse scattering of people, long soft shadows thrown towards camera. A person seen from behind
walks in through the entrance into the room, shoulders and the back of their head in the near
foreground. The stage lights sweep slowly, the haze drifts, people shift and talk in small groups.
The camera tracks slowly forward behind them.
```
锚点:**背对镜头**。人群**稀疏**(用户定过)。大屏当主光(上一轮平均亮度只有 15.4/255,太暗)。

### A7b · 签到牌(4s)

```
Same venue, same light. A tall free-standing sign near the entrance glows with a bright abstract
violet panel — a soft glowing square pattern of light with no readable characters. A person seen
from behind approaches it. Slow dolly-in past their shoulder towards the glowing panel.
```
那块"码"**只画成发光方块阵**,真码后期贴上去。

### A8 · 举手机扫码(5s)

首帧 = A7b 尾帧。

```
Unbroken continuation. The same person, still seen from behind, raises a phone in both hands up
towards the glowing panel. The camera moves in over their right shoulder, closing on the back of
the phone and then swinging round so the phone's glowing screen fills more and more of the frame.
The screen is an abstract violet gradient, no text.
```
